# Hi, I'm Rahul Tripathi 👋

## 🚀 About Me
I am a postdoctoral researcher specializing in computational biology, machine learning, and experimental molecular biology. My expertise spans high-dimensional (multi-omics) data integration, spatial transcriptomics, and the deployment of local LLMs for automated clinical data extraction. I focus on characterizing tumor immune microenvironments through WGCNA and spatial gradient analysis, complemented by a solid foundation in molecular cloning and pathway overexpression.

## 📊 Selected Projects & Research
*   **Automated Clinical Data Extraction Pipeline:** Deployed local LLMs (Ollama) to engineer an automated pipeline processing 501 unstructured prostate cancer pathology reports from TCGA to annotate Perineural Invasion (PNI) status.
*   **Multi-Omics Integration for Prostate Cancer:** Performed differential expression analysis via edgeR/limma and VST-normalized WGCNA to identify PNI-driven regulatory modules. Applied mixOmics to integrate WGCNA modules, miRNA, epigenetics, and protein data to dissect latent biological drivers underlying PNI.
*   **Neurological Disease Therapeutics (Ph.D. Research):** Engineered machine learning models using RDKit-derived molecular descriptors and recursive feature elimination to predict compound inhibitory activity. Evaluated binding stability and pharmacokinetics via virtual screening, 50 ns GROMACS molecular dynamics simulations, and deep learning-based blood-brain barrier predictions.
